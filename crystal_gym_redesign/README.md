# Johto Gym Leader Overhaul — Pokémon Crystal Recomp Mod

This mod gives each Johto Gym Leader a fuller five-Pokémon team. It drops the repeated filler picks, adds more matchup variety, and keeps the levels and moves closer to what Gen 2 expects.

Turn it on with `crystal_gym_redesign = true` in the `mods` section of `options.lua`, or use the F10 mod manager.

## Moveset Integrity Standard

Every move on every newly added Pokémon has been audited against its exact Gen 2 (Gold/Silver/Crystal) level-up learnset up to its assigned level:
- Invalid or premature level-up moves have been removed or backfilled with legal alternatives.
- Signature movesets (e.g., Whitney's Rollout Miltank, Clair's Dragonbreath Kingdra) are retained.
- Some TMs might have been added where appropriate.
